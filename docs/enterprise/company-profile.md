# ResTech — Company Profile

## 1. Organisation Overview

**Organisation Name:** ResTech

**Industry:** Technology Research, Engineering and Digital Services

**Organisation Type:** Medium-sized private technology company

**Primary Location:** Brisbane, Queensland, Australia

**Additional Locations:**

- Sydney, New South Wales
- Melbourne, Victoria
- Remote workforce across Australia

**Employees:** Approximately 500

**Contractors:** Approximately 50

ResTech is a fictional Australian technology company specialising in software engineering, applied research, cloud solutions, data analytics and technology consulting.

The organisation develops internal and customer-facing software systems and handles commercially sensitive research, intellectual property, customer information and employee information.

ResTech operates a hybrid technology environment consisting of cloud infrastructure, corporate networks, development environments, remote users and externally accessible services.

- User identity

## 9. Security Philosophy

ResTech adopts a Zero Trust security model.

Security decisions must be based on identity, device posture, application context, resource sensitivity and risk rather than simple network location.

ResTech assumes that:

- User accounts may become compromised.
- Endpoints may become compromised.
- Applications may contain vulnerabilities.
- Administrators may make mistakes.
- Credentials may be stolen.
- Attackers may gain an initial foothold.
- Internal networks may contain hostile activity.

The security architecture must therefore minimise attacker movement and reduce the consequences of individual security failures.

---

## 10. Security Objectives

ResTech's primary security objectives are:

- Protect sensitive information.
- Protect intellectual property.
- Prevent unauthorised access.
- Reduce implicit trust.
- Apply least privilege.
- Limit lateral movement.
- Protect privileged identities.
- Protect application and workload identities.
- Detect abnormal activity.
- Maintain comprehensive audit logging.
- Respond quickly to compromise.
- Recover critical services following security incidents.
- Maintain business continuity.
